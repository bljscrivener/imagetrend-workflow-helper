# Gremlin Logic A15 0.2.4.13

Cleaner blue/neutral panel with a small Help me guide. Routine view emphasizes Go; the redundant profile selector and idle Stop are hidden. Preview, timeline, legacy shortcuts, mapping and settings are under Tools / Troubleshooting. Copy diagnostic log and Nukacharta remain accessible in Tools. Nukacharta retains the existing two-confirmation clearing workflow and limited inventory.

Scene Delay: valid scene-arrival to departure intervals up to and including 20 minutes select None/No Delay; longer intervals select Staff Delay. Missing/invalid or negative intervals remain unresolved. Staff Delay must reflect the actual event; elapsed time does not establish cause.

Controlled tests cover threshold boundaries, missing/negative intervals, GUI help and navigation, hide/restore, native workflows and clear confirmation. Live verification after installing this release is still required.

Not included: automatic host-palette adaptation, Timestamp Lab or Crew Auditor integration, STEMI conflict repair, missing-procedure fixes, new PTA/access/ECG rules, or P3–P5 service classification. The separate micro-tools remain available. Legacy timestamp shortcuts are labeled unreliable rather than presented as fixed.
